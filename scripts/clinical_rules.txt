Healthcare AI Diagnostic Assistant — Clinical Rules for Automated Reasoning

These rules define the logical constraints that all AI diagnostic outputs must satisfy.
Any output that contradicts these rules is flagged as INVALID.

=== DIAGNOSTIC ACCURACY RULES ===

Rule 1: STEMI Diagnosis Requirement
If troponin is elevated (greater than 0.04 ng/mL) AND ST-elevation is present on ECG,
then Acute Myocardial Infarction MUST be included in the differential diagnoses.

Rule 2: Normal Cardiac Markers Exclusion
If troponin is normal (less than or equal to 0.04 ng/mL) AND ECG shows no ST changes,
then the primary diagnosis MUST NOT be Acute Myocardial Infarction.

Rule 3: Pulmonary Embolism Indicators
If the patient has a history of DVT AND presents with pleuritic chest pain AND D-dimer
is elevated (greater than 0.5), then Pulmonary Embolism MUST be included in the
differential diagnoses.

Rule 4: Age-Appropriate Risk Assessment
If the patient is 65 years or older, then cardiac risk factors MUST be assessed
and documented in the recommendation.

Rule 5: Pediatric Exclusion
If the patient is under 18 years old, then adult-specific diagnoses such as
Acute MI or Unstable Angina MUST NOT be the primary diagnosis without explicit
justification.

=== MEDICATION SAFETY RULES ===

Rule 6: Metformin Contraindication
If the patient is currently taking Metformin AND requires a contrast dye procedure
(such as CT Angiogram or cardiac catheterization), then the recommendation MUST
include holding Metformin.

Rule 7: Aspirin with Anticoagulants
If the patient is currently taking Warfarin AND Aspirin is recommended, then the
output MUST include a bleeding risk warning.

Rule 8: NSAID Contraindication in MI
If the diagnosis is Acute Myocardial Infarction, then non-aspirin NSAIDs MUST NOT
be recommended as they increase mortality risk.

=== CONFIDENCE AND SAFETY RULES ===

Rule 9: Low Confidence Mandatory Review
If the diagnostic confidence score is below 0.70, then the output MUST include
the statement "MANDATORY PHYSICIAN REVIEW REQUIRED" or equivalent warning.

Rule 10: High Confidence Ceiling for Complex Cases
If the patient has 3 or more active comorbidities AND is 75 years or older,
then the confidence score SHOULD NOT exceed 0.92 without comprehensive
laboratory confirmation.

Rule 11: Priority 1 Time Constraint
If the diagnosis is classified as Priority 1 (life-threatening), then the
recommended actions MUST include immediate intervention steps with a time
target (such as Door-to-Balloon less than 90 minutes for STEMI).

=== CONTROLLABILITY RULES ===

Rule 12: No Auto-Execution
The AI output MUST NOT recommend auto-executing any medication orders,
procedures, or treatments. All outputs are recommendations only.

Rule 13: Physician Override Acknowledgment
The output MUST include a disclaimer that it supplements and does not replace
physician clinical judgment.

Rule 14: Emergency Override Availability
If the case is flagged as Priority 1 or Priority 2, then the output MUST
indicate that emergency override mechanisms are available.

=== DATA COMPLETENESS RULES ===

Rule 15: Missing Data Disclosure
If critical data fields are missing (such as vital signs, lab results, or
medical history), then the output MUST explicitly state which data is missing
and how it affects diagnostic confidence.

Rule 16: Language Accessibility
If the patient's primary language is not English, then the output MUST note
this for the treating physician to ensure proper communication.
