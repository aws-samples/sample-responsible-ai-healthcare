Cardiac Risk Assessment Guidelines
Reference: Framingham Heart Study, ACC/AHA Risk Assessment 2019

FRAMINGHAM RISK SCORE:
Factors assessed:
- Age (points increase with age, especially > 55)
- Total cholesterol level
- HDL cholesterol level
- Systolic blood pressure
- Treatment for hypertension (yes/no)
- Smoking status (current smoker)
- Diabetes mellitus

Risk Categories:
- Low risk: < 10% 10-year cardiovascular event risk
- Moderate risk: 10-20% 10-year risk
- High risk: > 20% 10-year risk

TROPONIN INTERPRETATION:
- Normal: < 0.04 ng/mL (high-sensitivity assay)
- Borderline: 0.04 - 0.10 ng/mL (repeat in 3-6 hours)
- Elevated: > 0.10 ng/mL (suggests myocardial injury)
- Significantly elevated: > 1.0 ng/mL (high probability of MI)
- Markedly elevated: > 2.0 ng/mL (large MI, poor prognosis)

Serial troponin measurement:
- Rise and/or fall pattern required for acute MI diagnosis
- Repeat at 3 hours and 6 hours from presentation
- Delta change > 20% suggests acute process

ECG INTERPRETATION FOR ACUTE CORONARY SYNDROME:
- ST-elevation: Acute STEMI — emergent intervention required
- ST-depression: NSTEMI or unstable angina — urgent evaluation
- T-wave inversion: Ischemia — further workup needed
- Normal ECG: Does NOT rule out ACS (sensitivity ~50%)
- New LBBB: Treat as STEMI equivalent

CHEST PAIN RISK STRATIFICATION (HEART Score):
- History: Highly suspicious (2), Moderately suspicious (1), Slightly suspicious (0)
- ECG: Significant ST-deviation (2), Non-specific changes (1), Normal (0)
- Age: >= 65 (2), 45-65 (1), < 45 (0)
- Risk factors: >= 3 or history of atherosclerosis (2), 1-2 (1), None (0)
- Troponin: > 3x normal (2), 1-3x normal (1), Normal (0)

Score interpretation:
- 0-3: Low risk (< 2% MACE at 6 weeks) — consider discharge
- 4-6: Moderate risk — observation and further testing
- 7-10: High risk (> 50% MACE) — admit, aggressive management

D-DIMER INTERPRETATION (for Pulmonary Embolism):
- Normal: < 0.5 mg/L (age-adjusted: age/100 for patients > 50)
- Elevated: Suggests possible VTE but not specific
- High sensitivity (> 95%) but low specificity (~ 40%)
- Use with Wells Score or Geneva Score for clinical decision

WELLS SCORE FOR PULMONARY EMBOLISM:
- Clinical signs of DVT: 3 points
- PE most likely diagnosis: 3 points
- Heart rate > 100: 1.5 points
- Immobilization/surgery in past 4 weeks: 1.5 points
- Previous DVT/PE: 1.5 points
- Hemoptysis: 1 point
- Active cancer: 1 point

Interpretation:
- Low probability (0-1): PE unlikely, D-dimer to rule out
- Moderate probability (2-6): Further imaging needed
- High probability (> 6): CT pulmonary angiography indicated
