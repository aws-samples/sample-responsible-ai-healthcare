Medication Safety Guidelines for AI Diagnostic Assistant
Reference: FDA, AHA, ADA, Clinical Pharmacology Standards

CRITICAL DRUG INTERACTIONS AND CONTRAINDICATIONS:

1. METFORMIN:
   - HOLD before iodinated contrast procedures (CT angiogram, cardiac catheterization)
   - Risk: Lactic acidosis, especially with renal impairment
   - Hold 48 hours before AND after contrast administration
   - Resume only after renal function confirmed stable (eGFR > 30)
   - Also hold if: hemodynamic instability, sepsis, acute heart failure

2. ASPIRIN IN ACUTE MI:
   - GIVE immediately: 162-325mg chewed (not enteric-coated)
   - Safe with: ACE inhibitors, beta-blockers, statins
   - Caution with: Warfarin (increased bleeding risk — monitor INR)
   - Contraindicated if: Active GI bleeding, known aspirin allergy, severe thrombocytopenia

3. NSAIDs (Non-Aspirin) IN ACUTE MI:
   - CONTRAINDICATED in acute myocardial infarction
   - Increase mortality risk (AHA Class III — Harm)
   - Includes: Ibuprofen, Naproxen, Diclofenac, Celecoxib
   - Exception: Low-dose aspirin is NOT an NSAID for this purpose
   - If patient is on chronic NSAIDs: DISCONTINUE immediately in acute MI

4. BETA-BLOCKERS:
   - Indicated within 24 hours of MI (if no contraindications)
   - Contraindicated if: HR < 60, SBP < 100, severe asthma, acute heart failure (Killip III-IV)
   - Caution with: Calcium channel blockers (additive bradycardia)
   - Common agents: Metoprolol, Carvedilol, Bisoprolol

5. ACE INHIBITORS:
   - Start within 24 hours of MI (especially if EF < 40%)
   - Contraindicated if: SBP < 90, bilateral renal artery stenosis, pregnancy, angioedema history
   - Monitor: Potassium (hyperkalemia risk), creatinine
   - Caution with: Potassium supplements, potassium-sparing diuretics

6. ANTICOAGULANTS IN ACS:
   - Heparin (UFH): Weight-based dosing, monitor aPTT
   - Enoxaparin: Adjust for renal function (CrCl < 30: reduce dose)
   - Bivalirudin: Alternative if heparin-induced thrombocytopenia (HIT)
   - DO NOT combine multiple anticoagulants without clear indication

7. OPIOIDS (Morphine):
   - Use with caution in acute MI (may increase mortality per some studies)
   - Indicated for: Severe pain unresponsive to nitroglycerin
   - Monitor: Respiratory depression, hypotension
   - Naloxone should be available

8. NITROGLYCERIN:
   - Contraindicated if: SBP < 90, right ventricular MI, recent PDE5 inhibitor use (Viagra/Cialis within 24-48h)
   - Route: Sublingual first, then IV if persistent pain
   - Monitor: Blood pressure (can cause severe hypotension)

SPECIAL POPULATIONS — MEDICATION ADJUSTMENTS:

Elderly (> 75 years):
- Reduce anticoagulant doses (higher bleeding risk)
- Start medications at lower doses
- Monitor renal function more frequently
- Fall risk assessment before anticoagulation

Diabetic patients:
- Hold Metformin peri-procedure
- Monitor glucose closely (stress hyperglycemia common in MI)
- Insulin may be needed temporarily
- Avoid hypoglycemia (increases mortality in ACS)

Renal impairment:
- Adjust all renally-cleared medications
- Contrast-induced nephropathy prevention: IV hydration
- Avoid gadolinium if eGFR < 30
- Monitor electrolytes closely

MEDICATION RECONCILIATION REQUIREMENTS:
- Document ALL current medications before AI recommendation
- Check for interactions with proposed treatments
- Verify allergies and adverse reaction history
- Confirm medication adherence status
- Note any recent medication changes (within 30 days)
